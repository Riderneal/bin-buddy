# Impact & Benefits

## Impact on the target audience

Bin Buddy empowers urban residents to participate actively in waste management rather than
passively complaining about it. A resident who sees a dumping site can act on it in under a
minute — and gets rewarded, informed and acknowledged for doing so.

Local communities see the second-order effects: cleaner streets, fewer health hazards from
accumulated and burning waste, and a visible link between individual action and neighbourhood
outcomes. That link is what builds durable environmental habit, and it is exactly what most
civic waste programmes fail to create.

## Benefits by dimension

### 🌱 Environmental
- Diverts recyclable material away from landfill
- Brings **e-waste** into certified, compliant recycling channels instead of informal processing
- Reduces pollution from open dumping and waste burning
- Improves segregation quality at source, which raises recycling yield downstream

### 💰 Economic
- Generates revenue from the sale of sorted waste to recyclers
- Creates a reliable, pre-sorted supply stream for certified recyclers
- Provides income and predictable routing for local collectors and aggregators
- Reduces municipal landfill and collection costs through better routing

### 🤝 Social
- Creates and formalises jobs for waste collectors
- Supports NGOs and municipal bodies with data, reach and volunteer engagement
- Builds collective environmental consciousness and social responsibility
- Reduces health hazards in dense, underserved urban neighbourhoods

### 🧠 Technological
- Applies ML to a domain that is still largely manual and paper-driven
- Produces **hotspot analytics** that let cities optimise routes and target enforcement
- Builds an open, growing dataset of Indian urban waste imagery for future research
- Demonstrates a replicable model for citizen-sourced civic data

## Measuring success

| Metric | What it tells us |
|---|---|
| Reports per active user per month | Whether gamification is actually working |
| Report-to-pickup conversion rate | Whether the logistics layer can keep its promise |
| Tonnes diverted from landfill | Core environmental impact |
| Kg of e-waste routed to certified recyclers | Compliance and high-value impact |
| Repeat-hotspot reduction rate | Whether the analytics loop is changing city behaviour |
| Revenue per tonne collected | Unit economics and path to sustainability |
