# Problem Statement

**Theme:** Urban Infrastructure & Smart Cities
**Category:** Software
**Idea:** Bin Buddy — a smart waste management platform for urban India

## Context

India has a waste management problem. A significant share of the waste generated in urban
areas is never collected or recycled properly. This includes streams that are both valuable
and hazardous — most notably **e-waste**, which frequently ends up in landfills or informal
processing rather than in certified recycling channels.

## The gaps

### 1. No real-time visibility
Municipal bodies plan collection on fixed routes and fixed schedules. They have no live
picture of *where* waste is actually accumulating, so illegal dumping spots and overflowing
collection points persist for days before anyone acts.

### 2. No incentive for residents
Segregating waste and reporting dumping sites costs a resident time and gives them nothing
in return. Without an incentive, participation stays close to zero — and any system that
depends on citizen input fails at the first step.

### 3. A broken last mile
Local aggregators and kabadiwalas already exist and already work. But they operate on
word-of-mouth and fixed rounds, so they can't efficiently reach waste that is dispersed and
unpredictable. Certified recyclers, in turn, struggle to source a reliable, pre-sorted supply.

### 4. Economic value leaks out
Recyclable plastic, metal, paper and e-waste have real market value. When they go to landfill,
that value is destroyed — and the cost of landfilling is borne by the municipality on top of it.

## Who this hurts

| Stakeholder | Cost of the status quo |
|---|---|
| **Urban residents** | Unclean streets, health hazards, no way to act on what they see |
| **Municipalities** | Inefficient routing, higher landfill costs, no data to plan with |
| **Local aggregators** | Unpredictable supply, wasted trips, thin margins |
| **Certified recyclers** | Unreliable, unsorted input supply |
| **The environment** | Landfill growth, pollution, hazardous e-waste leakage |

## What a solution must do

1. Give residents a **reason** to participate, not just an ability to.
2. Convert scattered citizen reports into **structured, verified, actionable data**.
3. Plug into the **aggregators and recyclers that already exist** rather than replacing them.
4. Return **analytics to the city** so collection gets smarter over time.
5. Fund itself from the **value recovered from the waste**, not from user fees.

Bin Buddy is designed against exactly these five requirements.
